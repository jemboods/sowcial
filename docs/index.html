📖 How to Play

  1. Claim your farm — free. Connect a wallet that holds a Binance Agent ID and claim your 3×3 farm. One Agent ID = one farm, forever.
  2. Plant. Pick a plot and a crop: 🥬 Turnip (10 min), 🍈 Star Melon (1 hour) or 🎃 Pumpkin (6 hours). Every crop has its season — seasons rotate every 3 days.
  3. Water. Water a growing crop to multiply its harvest by ×1.5 Dewpoints.
  4. Harvest. When the timer finishes, harvest for Dewpoints and produce. In-season crops pay full points; out-of-season crops pay half and yield a little less.
  5. Help your neighbors. Visit other Agent ID farmers and help them — you both earn Dewpoints. Grow together!

Honest notes 🤝

  • No token. There is nothing to buy, sell, or speculate on.
  • Dewpoints are a non-transferable score stored in the game contract — they can’t be withdrawn or traded.
  • The contract holds no funds. It never custodies your money.
  • Playing costs only BNB Chain gas — a fraction of a cent per action at typical gas prices.
